# Agency and source marks

Agency and source marks belong to their owners. Sajag is a prototype and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

The marks below are used as-is: trimmed and resized only, never redrawn or recoloured. Most are not freely licensed (their own file pages say so); they stand for the agency or the data source they name, beside that name, and for nothing else. Where an agency has no mark on file (for example Mumbai Fire Brigade, ERSS 112, the ambulance (108) service), Sajag shows its own generic badge instead. Mumbai Traffic Police has no separate mark and is shown with the Mumbai Police badge. The Maharashtra State Disaster Management Authority has no mark on file; where the deck speaks of disaster response in general it shows the NDMA seal. Central Railway and Western Railway have no zone logos of their own and are both shown with the Indian Railways logo their sites carry.

- **mumbai-police** (Mumbai Police): non-free logo (en.wikipedia; file page licence: Fair use). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Mumbai_Police_logo.png. Blue star badge reading 'GREATER MUMBAI POLICE' round the scales of justice, with 'Brihanmumbai Police' in Marathi, the motto 'Sadrakshanaya Khalanigrahanaya' on a ribbon and the national emblem on top; the file page cites mumbaipolice.maharashtra.gov.in, and the force's site (mumbaipolice.gov.in) uses the same design as its header logo. Also stands for Mumbai Traffic Police, which has no separate mark on file (its site header shows only the State Emblem). Only 282x268 at source. Dark blue artwork on transparent: needs a light plate on dark grounds. Contains the State Emblem of India, whose use is restricted by the State Emblem of India (Prohibition of Improper Use) Act, 2005. Changes: trimmed, resized to at most 128 px tall, metadata stripped.
